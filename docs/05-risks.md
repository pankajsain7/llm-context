# F. Research Risks (ranked)

Status: 2026-10-19, revised after the Phase 2 critical review, the external audit (new Risk 6 on scoring validity; Risks 1, 3, 4, 9, 11, 13, and 14 updated), and **the second external audit** (new Risk 16 on the split-era confound and Risk 17 on test-split exposure; Risks 1, 6, 10, 11, and 13 updated). Ranking combines likelihood and impact on the *validity of the thesis conclusions*; practical risks are ranked by their potential to block completion. Evidence labels: **[V]** verified in this review, **[P]** from a full-text paper, **[A]** from an abstract, **[BK]** background knowledge (metadata verified, content not re-read), **[I]** interpretation.

## Ranked risk register

| # | Risk (validity type) | Evidence | Likelihood / impact | Mitigation | What to report |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Label noise interacts with context** (construct). SZZ-style labels mark changes that touch buggy lines, not necessarily changes that introduced the bug. Context can help a model spot *pre-existing* bugs, which inflates agreement with noisy labels | A formatting-only JIT-Defect4J commit labelled defective [V]. About half of SZZ bug-inducing commits were wrong in Herbold2022a [P via ReDef]. Ni2022 confirms plain `git blame` on manually validated fix lines, with no issue-date or whitespace filter named [P]. Label audit of 30 defective validation commits: [02-datasets.md](02-datasets.md) §2a | High / High | Gate 1 label-quality audit (30 defective validation commits). S2: exclude formatting-only commits. S8: high-confidence subset. Check whether rationales point to labelled buggy lines. Treat the label as an observed proxy in every claim | Label-noise discussion, audit result, subset results |
| 2 | **Weak signal and low power** (conclusion). Zero-shot LLM JIT-DP may be close to chance; condition differences may be smaller than run-to-run noise | ReDef: zero-shot Qwen2.5-7B after-only F1 < 0.1; fine-tuned F1 < 0.4 [P]. Size alone is a strong predictor [V]. Temperature 0 is not deterministic (Ouyang2025) [A]. With about 400 eligible defective commits the planning MDE is about 0.03–0.04 AUC, and larger for C2−C1 [I, design §8] | High / High | Census of eligible defective commits; threshold-free primary metric; a designated primary model; noise-floor repeats; equivalence tests with a *justified* SESOI; inconclusive results reported as such | MDE, noise floor, equivalence outcomes; negative results framed as findings |
| 3 | **Manipulation failure and retrieval construct validity** (construct). BM25 matches words, not defect relevance | BM25 retrieved sibling tests rather than the class under test in Example 2 [V]. With the fixed prototype, the top window for Example 1 was Base64 test data [V] | Medium / High | Token-bounded windows; manipulation check as a gate (automated proxy plus blinded human rating with κ and failure cases); pre-specified fallback: a `git grep`-anchored pool ranked by BM25 | Proxy statistics, κ, failure cases, retriever version used |
| 4 | **Training-data contamination and label memorization** (internal and external). It can be reduced, not excluded | The commits and their fixes have been public since 2001–2019, the benchmark labels since August 2022 (JIT-Fine `data.zip`) [V], and labels with code as plain JSON since July 2025 (JIT-Defect-Extended) [V]. Memorization of bug benchmarks varies by model (Ramos2025) [A] | High / Medium–High. It mostly inflates absolute performance; it can also interact with context, because more code means more recall cues | No messages or issue keys; document cutoffs (an earlier cutoff only avoids the most accessible copy); label-recall probe as *screening*, since a negative result proves nothing; flags for issue keys or later fixes in rationales; year stratification (S7); S10; claims limited to public historical commits | Cutoffs, probe result, contamination flags, year-stratified results, a cautious claim scope |
| 5 | **Overlap with prior work** (validity of the contribution). Snowball forward monthly until submission | Novelty matrix in the related-work chapter |
| 6 | **Scoring validity** (construct, measurement). The primary metric needs a valid score for both answers | Top-k log-probability lists do not guarantee that both YES and NO appear; support differs by provider and model, and reasoning models often return no probabilities [I; audit, provider documentation] | Medium / High | Hosted M1 chosen for exact two-candidate scoring (no local hosting, D12); equal logit bias on both answers where an API supports it; ≥ 98% valid-score gate in the pilot; pre-specified MCC fallback fixed before any test call; invalid responses never recoded | Scoring method and valid-score rate per model and condition |
| 7 | **Length, position, and prompt-format confounds** (internal) | Length and position effects (Levy2024, Liu2024) [BK]. | 8 | **Threshold shift mistaken for improvement** (conclusion). Context can make a model say YES more often without better discrimination | Inferred from metric definitions [I] | Low (the primary metric is threshold-free) / Medium | ROC-AUC as primary; predicted-positive rate, calibration curves, recall/FPR reported separately | Discrimination vs bias decomposition |
| 9 | **Selection bias from eligibility** (external) | Across all projects, a 16,000-token cap keeps 85–87% of defective train and validation commits; 12,000 keeps 80–83% and 8,000 keeps 72–76% [V]. The Phase 2 four-project estimate (93%) was too optimistic | High / Medium | Cap of 16,000 tokens; inference target defined as eligible Java changes; report exclusions by label and size; size-stratified results | CONSORT-style flow diagram of exclusions |
| 10 | **Model and API drift, and reproducibility** (internal and reliability) | Non-determinism (Ouyang2025) [A]; provider deprecations [I] | Medium / Medium | Pinned dated model IDs; system-fingerprint logging; response cache; **randomised request order across conditions** so drift is not confounded with condition; finish each model's run in a short window | Run log, dates, fingerprints, repeat agreement |
| 11 | **Engineering errors in context construction** | The external audit found three prototype defects (windows skipped up to 19 trailing lines, zero-score windows were selected, token matching not enforced); the second audit found two more (filling stopped at the first unfittable window; budgets were counted per snippet instead of on the rendered block). | 13 | **External validity, dataset dependence, and comparability** (external) | One benchmark: Java only, Apache projects only, code from 2001–2019 [V]. | 15 | **Scope creep** (practical): agentic retrieval, more conditions, fine-tuning | Common in LLM projects [I] | Medium / High | Out-of-scope list and decision log; changes only through the decision log, with your supervisor's agreement | Decision log |
| 16 | **Split-era confound** (internal, construct). ## Early-warning indicators (checked at Gates 2 and 3)

| Indicator | Threshold that triggers action | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Reconstruction failures | > 2% of sampled commits | Investigate the mapping; switch to the fallback dataset if the cause is structural |
| Invalid or unparseable outputs | > 2% | Tighten the output constraint or parsing on the validation split |
| Valid two-candidate scores (M1) | < 98% of pilot prompts | Switch to the pre-specified MCC fallback before any test call, or change the scoring method on validation data |
| Context blocks not `ok` (`line_limit`, `short`, `no_hits`) | Frequent in Gate 2 checks | Bound windows by tokens, revisit B (D9), and report the rates |
| C0 vs repeated-C0 flip rate | Comparable to the C0 vs C2 flip rate | Re-run the power analysis; consider more instances or a more deterministic model |
| Manipulation-check proxy | C2 ≤ C3 or a non-significant difference | Apply the pre-specified fallback retriever before any test run |
| Eligible defective commits | < 300 | Raise the token cap or use the fallback dataset |
| Pilot M1 ROC-AUC at C0 | ≤ 0.55 | Check the prompt and scoring; consider a stronger M1. A null-dominated study is still valid if you report it as such |
| Pilot correlation of scores between conditions | r < 0.7, so the MDE exceeds about 0.04 AUC | Agree with your supervisor on a larger SESOI, or on reporting the contrasts mainly as estimates with CIs; do not add conditions |
| Label-recall probe | Clearly above chance | Prefer a model with an earlier cutoff for M1, and report the probe result with every model |
| Projected cost | > agreed budget | Shrink the C3 seed count, the RQ3 series (subsample), and the M3 sample, in that order |
